Common questions

Do I pay to book?

No. Booking is free. We call you, look at the clock, and tell you the price before any work starts.

Which clocks do you visit at home?

Big ones: grandfather, wall and cuckoo clocks. Please give your home address when you book.

Can I book a watch repair online?

No. Please visit the store so we can look at your watch in person. Call us if you want to ask first.

How do I know when it is ready?

Use your booking ID and phone number on the Track page. We also call you when it is ready.
